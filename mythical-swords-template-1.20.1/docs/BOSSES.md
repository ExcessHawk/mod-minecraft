## Phase 1: Arthurian

### 👑 Rey Arturo
**Title:** King of Camelot  
**Health:** 500 HP (250 hearts)  
**Damage:** Very High  
**Mythology:** Arthurian Legend

#### Location
- Find Arthurian Castles in Plains or Forest biomes
- Use Camelot Compass to locate
- Minimum 240 blocks from spawn

#### Summoning
1. Find Boss Altar in castle throne room
2. Collect summoning items (TBD)
3. Right-click altar
4. Prepare for battle!

#### Drops
- **Guaranteed:**
  - Mythril Ingot (5-10)
  - Dragon Fang Fragment (2-3)
  - Sacred Iron Ingot (3-7)
- **Rare:**
  - Excalibur (5% chance)
  - Caliburn (10% chance)

#### Strategy
- High melee damage
- Powerful sword strikes
- Summons knights occasionally
- **Weakness:** Ranged attacks
- **Tip:** Keep distance, use bow

## Phase 4: Egyptian & Chinese

### 🐺 Anubis
**Title:** God of Death  
**Health:** 700 HP (350 hearts)  
**Damage:** Extreme  
**Mythology:** Egyptian

#### Location
- Enhanced Desert Temples in Desert biomes
- New pyramid-like structures
- Rare spawns in desert temples

#### Summoning
1. Find Boss Altar in enhanced desert temple
2. Collect summoning items:
   - 3x Gold Blocks
   - 5x Lapis Lazuli
   - 1x Nether Star
3. Right-click altar at night
4. Prepare for the judgment!

#### Drops
- **Guaranteed:**
  - Anubis Scale (3-6)
  - Sacred Iron Ingot (5-10)
  - Gold Ingot (10-15)
- **Rare:**
  - Khopesh (5% chance)
  - Scarab Amulet (10% chance)
  - Desert Glass (25% chance)

#### Abilities
- Death touch (instant wither)
- Summons mummy minions
- Life drain attacks
- Curse of decay aura

#### Strategy
- Fights best at night
- Extremely dangerous in close combat
- **Weakness:** DIVINE affinity weapons
- **Tip:** Keep distance, use ranged attacks
- Bring milk to remove curses

### ☀️ Ra
**Title:** Sun God  
**Health:** 750 HP (375 hearts)  
**Damage:** Extreme++  
**Mythology:** Egyptian

#### Summoning
Requires Boss Altar in desert temple + specific items during daytime

#### Drops
- **Guaranteed:**
  - Solar Fragment (2-4)
  - Gold Ingot (15-20)
  - Sacred Iron Ingot (7-12)
- **Rare:**
  - Was Scepter (5% chance)
  - Sun Disk (8% chance)
  - Solar Crown (3% chance)

#### Abilities
- Solar beam (massive fire damage)
- Flight capability
- Healing during day
- Burning aura
- Summons scarab swarms

#### Strategy
- **Day:** 150% damage, regenerates health
- **Night:** 50% damage, no regen
- **BEST STRATEGY:** Fight at sunset/night
- **Weakness:** ICE affinity, water
- **Tip:** Fire Resistance required

### 🐵 Sun Wukong
**Title:** Monkey King  
**Health:** 800 HP (400 hearts)  
**Damage:** Extreme++  
**Mythology:** Chinese

#### Location
- Celestial Palace structure (mountains)
- Cloud platforms
- Rare mountain biome spawns

#### Summoning
Find Boss Altar in Celestial Palace

#### Drops
- **Guaranteed:**
  - Golden Hair (4-8)
  - Cloud Fragment (2-5)
  - Celestial Bronze (5-10)
- **Rare:**
  - Ruyi Jingu Bang (3% chance)
  - Jade Essence (10% chance)
  - Immortality Peach (5% chance)

#### Abilities
- Creates 3 clones
- Extreme mobility/parkour
- Staff extends (knockback)
- Cloud surfing (flight)
- 72 transformations
- Becomes invincible briefly

#### Strategy
- HARDEST boss in mod
- Fast and unpredictable
- Clones deal real damage
- **Weakness:** AOE attacks, LIGHTNING affinity
- **Tip:** Full netherite, enchanted golden apples x10
- Multiplayer HIGHLY recommended

## 🎯 General Boss Tips

### Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Full diamond/netherite armor
- [ ] Enchanted weapons
- [ ] Golden apples (5+)
- [ ] Healing potions
- [ ] Strength/resistance potions
- [ ] Good food (steak, golden carrots)
- [ ] Backup gear in ender chest

### Combat Tips
1. **Learn patterns** - Each boss has tells
2. **Use terrain** - Castle walls, pillars
3. **Dodge, don't tank** - Most attacks one-shot
4. **Bring friends** - Multiplayer recommended
5. **Save progression** - Don't lose items

### Difficulty Ranking
1. **Easiest:** Loki
2. **Easy-Medium:** Rey Arturo, Oni Oscuro
3. **Medium:** Atenea, Quetzalcoatl
4. **Hard:** Susanoo, Odin, Anubis
5. **Very Hard:** Ra, Izanagi
6. **EXTREME (Hardest):** Sun Wukong
